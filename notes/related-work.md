# Related Work

This file is a working map of projects and research adjacent to Agent Society Lab. It is intentionally incomplete.

## Generative Agents

**Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior** — Park et al., 2023

https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442

A foundational demonstration of persistent LLM-based agents interacting in a shared simulated environment with memory, reflection, and planning.

## AgentSociety

https://github.com/tsinghua-fib-lab/AgentSociety

Tsinghua FIB Lab's AgentSociety project explores large-scale LLM-agent social simulation and agent-based social science.

Questions for comparison:
- How much agent identity is predefined?
- Which social structures are experimental inputs versus emergent outcomes?
- How are long-lived memories and agent histories represented?

## OASIS

https://github.com/camel-ai/oasis

OASIS, from the CAMEL-AI ecosystem, focuses on scalable social simulation with large populations of LLM agents.

Questions for comparison:
- What abstractions are required for large-scale simulation?
- Which abstractions remain useful at very small population sizes?
- How does agent lifecycle management affect long-running societies?

## Project Sid

https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00114

Project Sid studies large-scale agent civilizations in Minecraft-like environments and reports emergent social and cultural behavior.

## Artificial Life

Agent Society Lab also overlaps with questions traditionally studied in Artificial Life (ALife): emergence, open-endedness, evolutionary dynamics, artificial individuals, collective behavior, and cultural transmission.

## Open questions for the literature review

- What is the minimal mechanism required for persistent social differentiation?
- How should emergent specialization be operationally measured?
- How do we distinguish stable social structure from prompt-induced roleplay?
- What changes when agents share the same underlying model?
- What constitutes an agent's identity when the model itself is stateless?
- How should endogenous institutions be distinguished from environment rules?
